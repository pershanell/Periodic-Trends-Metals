OIL RIG · oxidation is loss, reduction is gain

Follow the electrons

Choose a reaction and move through the steps. Every electron lost must be gained elsewhere.

Electron transfer: choose each numbered step in order
Oxidation: loss of electrons; oxidation number increases. The electron donor is the reducing agent.
Reduction: gain of electrons; oxidation number decreases. The electron acceptor is the oxidising agent.

Oxidation numbers

RULE: The sum of all oxidation numbers equals the overall charge of the species. For a neutral compound the total is 0; for a polyatomic ion the total equals its charge. Count every atom (multiply its oxidation number by its subscript).
